Privacy Policy

RDF Media Uploader · last updated October 16, 2026

Who uses this application

RDF Media Uploader is a personal tool used only by its owner, Riccardo Dal Ferro, to manage the YouTube channels he owns. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission, granted through Google sign-in, the application accesses the owner's own YouTube channels through the YouTube Data API, only to upload videos, subtitles and thumbnails, and to manage titles, descriptions, playlists and publishing schedules.

It does not collect, store or process personal data of any other person.

Where data is stored

The authorization token issued by Google is stored only on the owner's computer. It is never shared, sold or sent to third parties. The videos and their information are sent only to YouTube.

How to revoke access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security settings: myaccount.google.com/permissions.

YouTube and Google

This application uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
